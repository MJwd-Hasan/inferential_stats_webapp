01 — WHO ARE WE TALKING ABOUT?

Population
vs. Sample

The population is the complete group we care about. The sample is the smaller subgroup we actually observe.

this smaller group is what we can actually measure
We measure a few
→ to learn about many.

03 — MAKE A BEST GUESS

Estimation

A point estimate is one number computed from the sample and used as our best guess for an unknown population parameter.

05 — PUT A CLAIM ON TRIAL

Hypothesis

We compare two competing claims about the population using sample evidence.

H₀

Null

The default or status quo — usually “no effect,” “no difference,” or “no change.”

VS

Would our sample look strange

06 — HOW SURPRISING IS THE DATA?

The
p-value

The p-value is the probability of seeing our sample result — or something more extreme — if the null hypothesis were entirely true.

this shaded tail
= this extreme
or more
small p

Typically < 0.05 → reject H₀ in favor of H₁.

large p

Not enough evidence to reject H₀.
